### Adelaide Transit

Live tracking for every Adelaide Metro bus, tram and train. I built it because the official app tells you when a bus is *scheduled*, and standing at a stop in the rain that's the wrong number.

<a href="https://github.com/RatterAU/AdelaideTransit">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RatterAU/AdelaideTransit/main/docs/demo.gif" width="220" align="left" alt="Adelaide Transit demo">
</a>

Tap a stop, see what's actually coming. Tap a departure, follow the bus itself — its speed, how many seconds ago it reported, every stop still ahead of it.

No accounts, no backend, no analytics. SwiftUI, on-device SQLite, straight off the public GTFS feeds.
